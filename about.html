Toyota Hybrid Technology

Toyota is the world's leader in sales of hybrid electric vehicles. Toyota encourages the mass-market adoption of hybrid vehicles across the globe. The company introduced the Toyota Prius in 1997. Its hybrid technology is called Hybrid Synergy Drive. This technology was later applied to many vehicles in Toyota's product lineup.
